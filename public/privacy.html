Privacy Policy — OnStage

Effective Date: August 19, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 30, 2026  ·  Developer: East Heights Labs LLC, Houston, TX

Overview

OnStage is a live music discovery app that shows you concerts and events happening near your current location. We built it to be simple and privacy-respecting. We don't require an account. We don't sell your data. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Information We Collect

Location Data

OnStage requests access to your device's location to show you events near you. Location is used only to query our event database — it is sent to our servers to fetch nearby shows and is not stored, logged, or shared with third parties.

You can use the app without granting location access by searching for a city manually.

Anonymous Device Identifier

OnStage generates a random identifier (UUID) the first time you launch the app. This identifier is:

  • Randomly generated — not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other personal information
  • Stored only on your device
  • Used to associate your stage time reports with your submissions so we can display community-reported data accurately
  • Never shared with advertisers or data brokers

You can reset this identifier by deleting and reinstalling the app.

Stage Time Reports (Optional)

If you submit a stage time report ("Report OnStage Time"), we collect the artist name, venue, date, and time you submit, along with your anonymous device identifier. We do not collect your name, email, or any contact information when you submit a report.

Event Data

OnStage displays event data from third-party sources including Ticketmaster and JamBase. We do not collect or store information about which events you view.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Apple ID or sign-in credentials
  • Precise location history or movement patterns
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data

How We Use Information

Data Purpose
Location (approximate) Fetch nearby events from our API
Anonymous device ID Associate stage time reports; prevent spam
Stage time reports Display community-reported show times to all users

Third-Party Services

OnStage uses the following third-party services to provide event data:

  • Ticketmaster Discovery API — event and venue data. Ticketmaster Privacy Policy
  • JamBase — live music event data, particularly for independent and jam band events. Powered by JamBase. JamBase Privacy Policy

Our servers are hosted on Vercel (Vercel Inc., San Francisco, CA). API requests including approximate location coordinates are processed by Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel Privacy Policy

Data Retention

  • Location data is not retained after your API request is fulfilled
  • Anonymous device IDs and stage time reports are retained to power community features. You may request deletion by contacting us.
  • We do not maintain user accounts or session logs

Children's Privacy

OnStage is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → OnStage
  • Reset your anonymous ID by deleting and reinstalling the app
  • Request deletion of any stage time reports you submitted by contacting us with the approximate date and venue

Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when our data practices change. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
