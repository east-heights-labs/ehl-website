2. Description of Service

ShowHunter is a live music discovery application that displays concerts, venue information, and community-reported stage times for events near your location. The App aggregates data from third-party sources and community contributions to help users find live music.

We do not sell tickets. We do not represent artists, venues, or promoters. Event information displayed in the App is provided for informational purposes and may not be complete or accurate.

2.1 Event Listing Completeness

ShowHunter aggregates event data on a commercially reasonable basis. A venue appearing in the App may show no upcoming events even when shows are scheduled. This can occur because:

  1. Ticketing platform gap: The venue is selling tickets through a platform or broker with which we do not have a data relationship.
  2. Unverified venue: The venue does not have a verified account with ShowHunter and has not been claimed through the ShowHunter Venue Dashboard.
  3. Verified but not updated: The venue has a verified ShowHunter account but has not updated its schedule in the Venue Dashboard to reflect current advertising or website listings.

We make no guarantee that event listings are complete, current, or representative of all shows at a given venue. Always confirm event details — including showtimes and ticket availability — directly with the venue or through the venue’s official website or ticketing provider.

3. Accounts and Identity

3.1 Anonymous Use

Core features of ShowHunter are available without creating an account. When you first launch the App, we assign a randomly generated device identifier (UUID) to your installation. This identifier is used to associate your activity — including favorites, “I’m Going” confirmations, and stage time reports — with your device. See our Privacy Policy for more detail on how this identifier is handled.

3.2 Sign In with Apple (Optional)

ShowHunter offers optional account creation through Sign In with Apple. If you choose to sign in, Apple will share a stable, anonymized account identifier (“Apple sub claim”) with us, which becomes your permanent account identity in ShowHunter. You may also optionally share your email address and display name with us through the Sign In with Apple flow; those fields are never required.

By signing in with Apple, you agree that we may: - Create a persistent ShowHunter account linked to your Apple sub claim - Optionally store your email address and display name if you choose to share them - Associate your prior anonymous activity (favorites, going, stage time reports) with your account, as described in Section 3.3 below - Retain your account data in accordance with Section 3.5 (Data Retention) and our Privacy Policy

We will never use your Apple ID credentials for any purpose other than account authentication within ShowHunter. We do not share your Apple ID or Apple sub claim with third parties.

3.3 Device UUID History and Activity Backfill

ShowHunter maintains a history of device UUIDs associated with your account. When you sign in with Apple, any prior anonymous activity associated with known device UUIDs — favorites, going confirmations, stage time reports — may be linked to your authenticated account. This backfill is intentional: it allows your history to persist across device reinstalls and new devices.

Specifically, we may retain: - All device UUIDs ever associated with your account, including first-seen and last-seen timestamps - Community contributions (stage time reports, going confirmations) linked to those device UUIDs - Favorites and saved venue/event data linked to those device UUIDs

If you do not sign in with Apple, your activity is associated only with the current installation’s UUID and is not persisted across reinstalls.

3.4 Tier and Entitlement Data

If you subscribe to ShowHunter or redeem a promo code, we maintain records of your subscription tier, tier source, entitlement grant history, and expiry dates. This information is linked to your account and is used solely to provision your subscription benefits. See Section 7 (Subscriptions and Billing) for details.

3.5 Account Deletion and Data Retention

You may request deletion of your ShowHunter account at any time by contacting us at privacy@eastheightslabs.com. In-app account deletion will be available in a future update. Upon account deletion:

  • Your Apple sub claim and any associated email/display name will be permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days
  • Your device UUID history and all associated activity (favorites, going, stage time reports) will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Your tier/entitlement records and promo code redemption history will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Exception: Community-contributed stage time reports may be retained in anonymized, de-identified form as part of our aggregate event accuracy data set, provided all linkage to your account or device UUIDs is severed

If you use ShowHunter anonymously (no Sign In with Apple), you may request deletion of your anonymous device UUID’s associated data by contacting us with your approximate activity window and city. Because anonymous UUIDs are not linked to any personal account, we cannot guarantee precise attribution, but will make reasonable efforts to fulfill your request.
