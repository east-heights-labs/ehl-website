Account Deletion

To delete your ShowHunter account and all associated data, go to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you're unable to access the app, email support@eastheightslabs.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and we will process it within 30 days.

Subscription & Billing

ShowHunter subscriptions are managed by Apple through the App Store. To cancel or manage your subscription, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Apple handles all billing inquiries. For refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
